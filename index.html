Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Chroma Palette is designed to let you create color palettes, gradients, and contrast checks directly in your browser. This template does not include a website account system or a custom server that receives your color selections.

1. Information this website processes

The palette generator, color picker, gradient builder, and contrast checker process the colors you enter in your browser so the tools can work. This single-file version does not send those color values to a Chroma Palette server.

If you save favorite palettes, this page uses your browser's local storage to keep them on your device. Clearing browser data or using another browser or device may remove or not show those saved items.

2. Cookies and local storage

This template uses local storage for saved palettes and session storage to remember whether the animated intro has been dismissed during the current browser session. It does not itself set advertising cookies or include analytics tracking code. Your hosting provider, browser, or other services you later add may process technical information under their own policies.

3. Advertising and third-party services

No advertising network is integrated into this starter file. If you later add Google AdSense, analytics, embedded content, affiliate links, or other third-party services, update this policy to accurately explain what those services collect or store, how they use cookies or similar technologies, and how visitors can manage relevant settings. Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. How information is used

Color values are used to provide the tool features you request. Saved palettes are stored locally to make them available on a later visit in the same browser. This template does not provide a contact form or collect information through a Chroma Palette account.

5. Children's privacy

This website is a general-purpose creative tool. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not add a form or other feature that collects personal information without first considering applicable privacy laws and safeguards.

6. Data security and your choices

Local browser storage is controlled by your browser and device. You can remove saved palettes by using the Clear favorites button or clearing this site's local storage in your browser. No method of online service or local storage can be promised to be completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may need to change when the website's features, hosting, analytics, or advertising change. Review it regularly and update the date and wording so it reflects the live website.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, add a real contact email address or another reliable contact method here before publishing the site widely. Do not leave placeholder contact information or claim to offer a contact service that does not exist.
